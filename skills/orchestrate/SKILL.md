It binds its v5 receipt to a physically bound separate host-local anchor ancestry, and the installed command authenticates the receipt's persisted reviewed-checkout path and source digest through that anchor instead of deriving a source path from `site-packages`. Its source-, archive-, anchor-, and command-bound healthy fast path is silent and write-free and requires the dedicated user-PATH entry first.
